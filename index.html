06 — Method

Assumptions & data notes

  • Budget = one month, all-in: SAR 160,000 = social 130,000 + Google 30,000.
  • New Khamis Mushait branch (Dental only): SAR 25,000 = 20,000 Dental leads (Snapchat · TikTok · Meta) + 5,000 opening awareness (Snapchat · TikTok · Meta), funded by SAR 15,000 from the Riyadh allocation plus an additional SAR 10,000 (total budget SAR 160,000). Campaigns are geo-targeted around the branch. Lead projections use each platform's September average and are approximate; awareness impressions use September CPMs (Snapchat 18.35 · TikTok 9.99 · Meta 8.52 SAR).
  • Baseline spend = 7–30 Sep 2026 ND 96 exports (Snapchat USD × 3.75; TikTok & Meta native SAR). Leads & bookings = CRM leads 10–30 Sep (CRM data as of 11 Oct) plus bookings recorded for branch-handled Derma leads. TikTok 7–15 Sep spend is allocated to its Dental Riyadh / Madinah campaigns. Booking = "Booked" in Reached OR WA-Results; "Already Booked" excluded.
  • Google results are approximate. Maps & Calls can start now; Search (Brand + Dental + Derma) is on hold until its set-up is complete. Search & Brand: CPL SAR 60, booking rate 30%. Maps & Calls (clicks on Call / Directions, not actual calls or visits): 60% call clicks at SAR 20, 40% direction clicks at SAR 6. No account history behind these.
